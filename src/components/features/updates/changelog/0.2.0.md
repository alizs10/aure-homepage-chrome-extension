## New Features

### Search Engine Selection

- **Default Search Engine**: Choose your favorite search engine from Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Bing, Yandex, Gerdoo, or ChatGPT directly in Settings.
- **Smart Query Routing**: Web queries and search completions from your homepage search bar instantly route through your chosen provider.

### Quick Search Engine Commands

- **On-the-Fly Engine Queries**: Type `@` in the homepage search bar to browse available search engines, or type `@<engine>` (such as `@chatgpt`, `@brave`, `@duckduckgo`, `@bing`, etc.) followed by your query to search with a specific engine without changing your default settings.
- **Dynamic Suggestions**: Live search suggestions instantly adapt to your targeted engine command as you type.

### Configurable Tab Behaviors

- **Open Search in New Tab**: Easily toggle whether web searches from your homepage open in a new tab or the current tab.
- **Open Bookmarks in New Tab**: Configure bookmarks, favorites, and folder shortcuts to open in a new tab instead of replacing your homepage.

## Improvements

### Bookmarks Settings

- **More Organized**: Bookmarks settings are now more organized, making it simpler to manage your shortcuts, folders, and display options.

### Pomodoro Daily Summary

- **Visual Time Breakdown**: The Daily Summary modal now features a multi-segment circular progress ring around your total focus time, cleanly separating your focus sessions, short breaks, and long breaks at a glance.

## Bug Fixes

### Network Status IP Address

- **Clean IPv4 Display**: The IP checker now prioritizes standard IPv4 addresses, ensuring your public IP displays cleanly and compactly in the header status widget instead of lengthy IPv6 addresses.

### Firefox Smoothness

- **Smoother Hover Experience**: Refined bookmark and shortcut animations to make the hover experience feel much smoother and more responsive in Firefox.
