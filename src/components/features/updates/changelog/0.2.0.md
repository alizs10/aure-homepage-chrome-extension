## New Features

### Search Engine Selection

- **Default Search Engine**: Choose your favorite search engine from Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Bing, Yandex, Zarebin, Gerdoo, or ChatGPT directly in Settings.
- **Smart Query Routing**: Web queries and search completions from your homepage search bar instantly route through your chosen provider.

### Configurable Tab Behaviors

- **Open Search in New Tab**: Easily toggle whether web searches from your homepage open in a new tab or the current tab.
- **Open Bookmarks in New Tab**: Configure bookmarks, favorites, and folder shortcuts to open in a new tab instead of replacing your homepage.

## Improvements

### Bookmarks Settings

- **More Organized**: Bookmarks settings are now more organized, making it simpler to manage your shortcuts, folders, and display options.

## Bug Fixes

### Firefox Smoothness

- **Smoother Hover Experience**: Refined bookmark and shortcut animations to make the hover experience feel much smoother and more responsive in Firefox.
