## New Features

### Multi-Browser Support
* **Firefox & Chrome**: Aure Homepage is now officially available on Mozilla Firefox alongside Google Chrome with complete feature parity and smooth performance.
* **Smart Icon Caching**: Cross-browser high-resolution favicon caching with automatic offline fallbacks.

### Real-Time Cross-Tab Synchronization
* **Seamless Multi-Tab Experience**: Any changes made in one tab instantly reflect across all open tabs without refreshing.
* **Settings & Layout**: Appearance settings, theme accents, and dashboard arrangements stay synchronized in real time.
* **Bookmarks**: Adding, editing, and organizing Favorites and Folders updates across every open tab immediately.
* **All Widgets in Sync**: Notes & Tasks, Counters, Progresses, Mood Tracker, Pet House, and Calendar keep your data aligned everywhere.
* **Custom Wallpapers**: Wallpaper changes apply instantly across all open tabs.

### Live IP & Network Details
* **Public IP & Country Flag**: View your public IP address and country flag directly from the Network Status widget.
* **Privacy Controls**: A new Privacy tab in Settings lets you toggle IP inspection on or off anytime.

### Progress Goals Details & Sorting
* **Detailed Goal Modal**: A new detail modal displaying full info and all actions for your progress cards.
* **Custom Goal Sorting**: Easily sort your progress goals by Priority (High to Low / Low to High) or Date Created (Newest / Oldest first), with your choice remembered.

### Mood Tracker
* **Missed Days**: You can now choose your mood for missed days directly in the tracker grid.

## Improvements

### Pomodoro Timer Safeguards
* **Action Confirmations**: Helpful confirmation prompts before resetting a cycle or session, taking early breaks, or managing overtime prevent accidental progress loss.

### Counters & Countdowns
* **Expired Countdown Tracking**: Expired countdowns now display negative elapsed days (e.g., -3d) so you know exactly how many days have passed.
* **Refined Resetting**: Recurring countdowns and count-ups can be reset with a single click while fixed milestones stay protected.

### Notes & Checklists
* **Smart Multi-Line Paste**: Pasting or dropping multi-line text into the quick note input automatically converts it into a multi-line note, preserving your line breaks and cursor position.

### Pet House
* **Quick Confirmation Copy**: Click-to-copy helpers in pet deletion and release dialogs make confirming actions fast and hassle-free.

### Bookmarks Experience
* **Clearer Navigation**: Renamed "Sites & Folders" to "Bookmarks" in Settings for a more natural, familiar experience.

### Performance & Visual Polish
* **Lighter & Faster**: Extracted clock rendering and streamlined core architecture for lower memory usage and smoother transitions.
* **Consistent Dates**: Standardized date and time formatting throughout all widgets for a clean look.
* **New Wallpapers**: New default wallpapers have replaced the old ones.

### Alarms & Notifications
* **Notification Focus**: Clicking timer notifications reliably unminimizes and brings your browser window into focus.

## Bug Fixes

### Search
* **Search Suggestions**: Fixed search suggestions popping up unexpectedly when switching back to an open tab.
