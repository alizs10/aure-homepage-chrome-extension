## New Features

### Real-Time Cross-Tab Synchronization

* **Seamless Multi-Tab Experience**: Any changes made in one tab instantly reflect across all open tabs without refreshing.
* **Settings & Layout**: Appearance settings, theme accents, and dashboard arrangements stay synchronized in real time.
* **Bookmarks**: Adding, editing, and organizing Favorites and Folders updates across every open tab immediately.
* **All Widgets in Sync**: Notes & Tasks, Counters, Progresses, Mood Tracker, Pet House, and Calendar keep your data aligned everywhere.
* **Custom Wallpapers**: Wallpaper changes apply instantly across all open tabs.

## Improvements

### Performance & Stability

* **Lighter & Faster**: Streamlined core architecture for quicker startup, smoother transitions, and lower memory usage.
* **Consistent Dates**: Standardized date and time formatting throughout all widgets.

### Alarms & Notifications

* **Notification Focus**: Clicking timer notifications reliably brings your browser window into focus.

## Bug Fixes

### Search

* Fixed search suggestions popping up automatically when switching back to an open tab.
